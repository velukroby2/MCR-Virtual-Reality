Phone headset mode

For Google Cardboard / JioDive: use your Render HTTPS link, allow motion access, rotate to landscape, then insert your phone. Stay seated. Hold your gaze on a control for 1.3 seconds to press it; look away before pressing it again.

Device graphics

Maximum quality is the default. If the phone becomes warm or head turns begin to stutter, try 85% resolution, 2× smoothing and medium shadows. Optics defaults are 75° FOV, 0.08 correction and 64 mm eye spacing. If straight lines bend or “swim,” adjust correction; if the image feels doubled, adjust eye spacing. Stop immediately if uncomfortable.
