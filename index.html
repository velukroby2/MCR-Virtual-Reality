Phone headset mode

For Google Cardboard / JioDive: use your Render HTTPS link, allow motion access, rotate to landscape, then insert your phone. Stay seated. Hold your gaze on a control for 1.3 seconds to press it; look away before pressing it again.

Comfort-first defaults use a narrower view, mild lens correction and standard 64 mm eye spacing. If the image bends while you turn, adjust lens correction; if it feels doubled or strains your eyes, adjust eye spacing. Set correction to 0 for a flat preview. Stop immediately if uncomfortable.
